Programs fail in the seams, between strategy and execution, between systems and people, between executive intent and frontline delivery. Our program management practice builds the connective tissue. Project management is what you do once a program is structured. We do the structuring.

Capabilities

What we actually deliver.

Eight distinct capabilities. Every engagement is sized and configured to your context, never a templated rollout.

1

PMO Design & Stand-Up

From charter through staffing model through operating cadence, building project management offices that are accountable, not bureaucratic.

2

Program Governance Frameworks

Steering committees, working groups, escalation protocols, and decision-rights architectures aligned to program risk.

3

Portfolio Management

Multi-program portfolio governance, prioritization frameworks, and resource allocation models for executive committees.

4

Stage-Gate Review Design

Phase-gate methodologies (DMAIC, SAFe, PMBOK-aligned) calibrated to your delivery context.

5

Risk & Issue Management

Risk register design, mitigation planning, and the executive review cadence that keeps risks visible and owned.

6

Schedule & Milestone Management

Integrated master schedules, critical-path analysis, and milestone reporting for programs with hard external deadlines.

7

Resource Planning & Allocation

Capacity models, resource leveling, and contractor/government workforce integration.

8

Program Reporting & Dashboards

Executive dashboards, status reporting protocols, and the data architecture that makes them accurate.

How We Work

Our methodology, applied to this practice.

1

Charter

Define program scope, success criteria, governance, and accountability structures before a single deliverable is built.

2

Structure

PMO design, integrated master schedule, risk register, and the governance cadence that ties them together.

3

Execute

Stand up the PMO, run the cadence, manage to the schedule, and escalate the risks. We do the work, your team learns it.

4

Transfer

Transition the PMO to your internal leadership with playbooks, training, and a defined handoff plan.
